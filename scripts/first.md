# Reel script v2: "Should you believe everything you watch?"

**Format:** Instagram Reel, 1080×1920, 30 fps, MP4 (H.264 + AAC)
**Target length:** about 27 s. The invite CTA adds about 2 s to the original 25 s plan.
**Look:** the website's dark "invert" palette. Pure black `#000`, white `#fff`, greys `#c4c4c4` / `#5a5a5a` / `#1c1c1c`, hard offset shadows in `#6d6d6d`. No other colours, same as the site.
**Type:** Archivo Variable (the site font). Headlines at weight 900, condensed (`font-stretch: 62%`), uppercase, tight leading (.86). Key words use the site's outlined style. Labels at 800 weight, uppercase, letter-spaced.
**Recurring motifs from the site:** 3px white borders, hard offset shadows, the rotated verdict stamp, the verdict glyphs (✕ ✓ ◐ ? ↻ —), the scrolling ticker, the diagonal-stripe reel thumbnails.
